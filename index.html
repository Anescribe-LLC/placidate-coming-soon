Privacy Policy

Effective: October 6, 2026

At PLACIDATE™, privacy is built into the authentication process. PLACIDATE™ is an authentication and account-recovery service developed by Anescribe, L.L.C. for electronic medical record systems and other authorized systems.

This Privacy Policy describes how PLACIDATE™ handles information when a user scans the PDF417 barcode on a U.S. driver's license or other supported government-issued identification.

1. Information Contained in Your ID Barcode

The PDF417 barcode on a government-issued ID may contain considerably more information than PLACIDATE™ needs to provide authentication.

Depending on the issuing jurisdiction, that information may include a person's name, full date of birth, residential address, physical characteristics, driver's-license information, and other identifying data.

PLACIDATE™ is designed to minimize the information retained from your identification barcode.

2. Information PLACIDATE™ Retains

PLACIDATE™ is designed to retain only the information needed for its authentication function:

  • Your name; and
  • Your birthday, limited to the month and day of birth.

PLACIDATE™ does not intentionally retain your year of birth.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Retain

PLACIDATE™ is designed not to retain information from your ID barcode that is unnecessary for authentication, including:

  • Your residential or mailing address;
  • Your year of birth;
  • Your weight;
  • Your hair or eye color;
  • Your height or other physical descriptors;
  • Your driver's-license number;
  • Your driver's-license class, restrictions, or endorsements; or
  • Driving history, traffic violations, or similar driving-record information.

Some of these data elements may be read or processed transiently when the barcode is scanned and decoded. PLACIDATE™ is designed to discard information that is not required for its authentication function rather than store it as part of your PLACIDATE™ record.

4. How We Use Retained Information

Information retained by PLACIDATE™ is used only to provide, maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve authentication and account-recovery services.

We do not sell information obtained through PLACIDATE™.

We do not use driver's-license or identification information for advertising or third-party marketing.

5. EMRs and Other Integrating Systems

PLACIDATE™ may be integrated into an electronic medical record system, website, application, or other service operated by another organization.

That organization may separately collect, store, or process information about you. Its handling of information is governed by its own privacy practices and is outside the control of PLACIDATE™ and Anescribe, L.L.C.

Organizations integrating PLACIDATE™ are responsible for appropriately configuring and securing their own systems and for complying with applicable privacy, security, employment, professional, and other legal requirements.

6. Data Security and Data Minimization

PLACIDATE™ uses technical and administrative safeguards designed to protect information processed through the service.

An important part of our security approach is data minimization: information that PLACIDATE™ does not need is designed not to be retained.

No software, device, network, or data-processing system can guarantee absolute security. Although PLACIDATE™ is designed to discard unnecessary information contained in an identification barcode, we cannot guarantee that such information could never be unintentionally exposed as the result of a software defect, compromised device, unauthorized access, security incident, third-party system, or other circumstance.

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, Anescribe, L.L.C. is not responsible for unauthorized disclosure caused by an integrating organization's systems, a user's device or conduct, third-party systems outside our control, or other circumstances outside our reasonable control.

Nothing in this Privacy Policy limits any obligation or liability that cannot lawfully be limited or excluded.

7. Data Retention

PLACIDATE™ retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide authentication and account-recovery services, maintain security and audit records, support users, comply with applicable legal requirements, resolve disputes, and protect the service against misuse.

Information that PLACIDATE™ is not designed to retain is not intentionally maintained as part of the user's PLACIDATE™ authentication record.

8. No Sale of Personal Information

PLACIDATE™ does not sell personal information obtained through identification scans.

Information obtained through PLACIDATE™ is not shared with third parties for third-party advertising or marketing.

9. United States Only

PLACIDATE™ is intended for use in the United States with supported U.S. government-issued identification.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as PLACIDATE™ develops. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
